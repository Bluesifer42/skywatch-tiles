Skywatch privacy policy

Last updated 6 October 2026. Skywatch is an educational telescope game and sky guide published by Bluesifer. This page explains what the app does with your data.

What the app collects

Location. With your permission, the app reads your device location (approximate or precise, your choice) to work out what is in your sky, when it is dark, and to fill in the mount set-up card. The location is stored only on your device.

Weather. To fetch a cloud forecast, the app sends your latitude and longitude (and those of any in-game observatory site) to the Open-Meteo weather service over an encrypted connection. Open-Meteo does not require an account and the request carries no other identifier. See open-meteo.com for their terms.

Sky events and imagery. The app downloads a public calendar of sky events and public telescope imagery from our own web hosting. These downloads carry no personal data.

Optional briefing. If a briefing service is configured in a future version, the app may send a short fact sheet (your site rounded to two decimal places, tonight's sky numbers, your described telescope) to that service to have the words written. No account, name or contact detail is included. The current version has no such service enabled.

What the app does not do

  • No accounts, sign-in, names or email addresses.
  • No advertising SDKs and no analytics or crash-reporting services.
  • No selling or sharing of data with third parties beyond the weather request described above.
  • Your game progress, telescope profile and preferences stay on your device.

Permissions

Location: sky calculations, weather, mount set-up. Notifications: optional sky alerts you turn on in Settings; scheduled on the device. Sensors: the level and compass screen uses the accelerometer and magnetometer while that screen is open; readings are not stored.

Deleting your data

Uninstalling the app removes everything it stored. Nothing is kept on our servers.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and collects no personal information from anyone.

Contact

Questions about this policy: open an issue at github.com/Bluesifer42/skywatch-tiles.
